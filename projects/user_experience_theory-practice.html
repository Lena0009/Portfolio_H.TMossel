UX Theory and Practice

U&S C&A

In this course, I learned how interactive and intelligent systems can be designed to create positive and meaningful user experiences. I developed an understanding of how products, systems, and services shape user experience in everyday life, while also strengthening my empathy skills to better understand users’ needs and design effective solutions.

Throughout the course, I gained knowledge of key UX concepts, frameworks, and evaluation methods, including the User Experience Questionnaire (UEQ). I applied this knowledge in a hands-on industry challenge provided by Bureau Moeilijke Dingen. Through this course I was able to form my vision of what User Experience Design is and how it shapes my own vision.

Activity Information:

  • Institution: Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)
  • Course: User Experience Theory and Practice (DDM150)
  • Year: 2023
  • Team: Amna Strojil, Rosa van Wershoven, Stefanie Zins, Helena Mossel
