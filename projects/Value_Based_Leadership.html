Value Based Leadership

B&E C&A

In this course, I learned how design leadership extends beyond end users to engaging diverse stakeholders inside and outside organizations. Through projects with Jumbo, Catharina Hospital, and PostNL, I applied frameworks such as the Three Horizon Framework and Situational Leadership Framework to understand innovation and value creation across individuals, teams, organizations, and external stakeholders.

The course strengthened my skills in stakeholder alignment, collaboration, communication, and team leadership, while helping me reflect on my professional development. It also deepened my understanding of responsible design leadership through trust, shared leadership, and leading by example.

Activity Information:

  • Institution: Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)
  • Course: Values based leadership in business innovation (DAM170)
  • Year: 2023
  • Team: Pin Yuang Wang, Tessa van Maanen, Harry Wang, Helena Mossel
