Wegwijzer is a self-reflection and conversational tool designed to help Havo and VWO students choose their subject profiles at the age of 14–15. Using three card decks focused on personality (Who am I?), interests and preferences (What do I like?), and career vision (What do I find interesting?) students create a visual map of their responses that supports reflection and discussion with peers and mentors. Beyond guiding profile choices, the project encourages students to better understand themselves and reflect on how their decisions can shape their future.

Project Information:

  • Institution: Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)
  • Course: Squad Project Pre-Master (DEP005)
  • Year: 2023
  • Team: Giovanni Sapienza, Helena Mossel
  • Experts/Stakeholder: Roncalli Scholengemeenschap
  • Exibition: Presented in Night of the Nerds and University of the Future

What did I learn...

This project was a challenging but valuable learning experience that taught me important lessons in leadership, communication, and teamwork. Taking on a management role exposed me to difficult group dynamics and pushed me to reflect on my own leadership style, helping me become more open-minded and balanced in my expectations.

The project also helped me expand my skills across different areas. I gained experience with stakeholder mapping, interviews, user testing, and strategic tools such as value propositions and SWOT analysis. On the technical side, I worked with Jupyter for data acquisition and visualization, while also using my graphic design background to create an engaging and visually appealing tool. Overall, the project challenged me in unexpected ways and became an important learning experience in my growth as a designer.
