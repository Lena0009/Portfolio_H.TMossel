Colabora is a crowdsourcing app-game designed to improve local pollen forecasting for hay fever sufferers. By motivating non-allergic users to scan and discover trees through playful collection and exploration mechanics, the project creates a local dataset of tree species and locations that contributes to an Urban Digital Twin of local flora. Combined with bloom data and local weather information, this data can be used to generate more accurate, location-specific pollen forecasts and help users with hay fever, better manage their symptoms.

Project Information:

  • Institution: Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)
  • Course: M1.1 Design Project
  • Year: 2024
  • Team: Kaixuan Chen, Roy Op het Veld, Şevval Bayrak, Helena Mossel

What did I learn...

This project strengthened my skills across business, design, and development. I used stakeholder mapping and value propositions to explore opportunities, and deepened my user involvement through interviews, co-creative sessions, and user testing to support iteration.

I focused on embracing process over outcome through ideation and prototyping, learning to balance open exploration with clearer boundaries between exploration and execution. On the technical side, I followed a self-directed C++ course and applied it in Unity to build a basic 2D prototype with a QR scanner, improving my debugging skills.

As project lead, I organised planning and sprints using weekly goals and a kanban board, applying a democratic leadership style. This improved my communication and coordination skills, while also highlighting the challenge of balancing inclusivity with fast decision-making.
