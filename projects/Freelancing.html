During my Pre-Master’s and Master’s studies, I worked as a freelance designer for Estivant Vakanties and Games for Health Europe.

At Estivant Vakanties, I supported the marketing team in developing a new house style, focusing on visual exploration and brand identity. For Games for Health Europe, I worked on multiple multidisciplinary projects (Etain, Dice, Budiyu, Amgen, ZIN), contributing to both concept development and visual design across all projects.

Through these experiences, I developed a range of skills. I strengthened my brainstorming and ideation abilities, learning to communicate ideas clearly, support my arguments, and build on others’ input. I improved my ability to translate abstract concepts into visual designs and further developed my prototyping skills through iterative work. I also enhanced my communication skills by working with diverse stakeholders such as project managers, developers, and designers. In addition, I developed confidence in standing behind and selling my work as a freelancer. Finally, I strengthened my organizational skills by balancing freelance projects with full-time studies, managing expectations, structuring my workload, and meeting deadlines.
