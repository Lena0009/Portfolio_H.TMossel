During this course, I learned how to design technologies that support responsible, non-coercive behaviour change. I examined the relationship between six psychological theories, design principles, and ethical considerations, and applied these concepts in a real-world design case developed by my team and me. Building on the knowledge gained in the course, I had the opportunity to apply these ideas in the case study “Bin the Butt,” which focused on investigating the littering behaviour of cigarette butts among student smokers on the smoke-free TU/e campus.

U&S C&A

During this course, I learned how to design technologies that support responsible, non-coercive behaviour change. I examined the relationship between six psychological theories, design principles, and ethical considerations, and applied these concepts in a real-world design case developed by my team and me. Building on the knowledge gained in the course, I had the opportunity to apply these ideas in the case study “Bin the Butt,” which focused on investigating the littering behaviour of cigarette butts among student smokers on the smoke-free TU/e campus.
