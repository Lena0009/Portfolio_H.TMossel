Seavilization

C&A T&R M,D&C

Seavilization reframes the challenges of rising sea levels by imagining a speculative future in which humans coexist with water. Using augmented reality and gamification, the project invites visitors to shape their own “Seavilization,” lowering the tension around climate transition while sparking curiosity about alternative futures.

The installation features an interactive sandbox equipped with a Kinect 2 and Arduino Uno, which detect height variations to generate real-time projected environments. Depending on the landscapes visitors create, different elements emerge, such as fish farms, floating cities, and hybrid urban ecosystems. Three buttons placed on the side also allow visitors to simulate rising sea levels and observe how their creations adapt to a changing climate.

Project Information:

  • Institution: Design Academy Eindhoven (DAE)
  • Course: Final Bachelor Project
  • Year: 2022
  • Featured: Graduation Show DDW22

What did I learn...

This project strengthened my project management and organizational skills, while helping me consolidate the design process I developed over four years at DAE. It pushed me to trust my design instincts and hands-on approach in shaping Seavilization.

I gained practical experience working with wood and metal, developing my skills in welding and woodworking, while also expanding my programming abilities by building the augmented reality experience with a Kinect 2 and Arduino Uno. Finally, the graduation process sharpened my presentation skills, culminating in presenting the project at the DDW22 Graduation Show.
