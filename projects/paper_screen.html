Paper Screen

U&S C&A T&R M,D&C

Paper Screen explores a sustainable, paper-based alternative to LCD screens by using light and tangible interaction instead of traditional displays. The project investigates how embedded ArUco markers and light-tracking via wearable light sources can transform paper into an interactive surface for controlling digital content.

Users interact by shining light onto layered paper, with a camera system translating illuminated areas into cursor-based actions such as moving, rotating, and scaling objects. User testing showed the concept to be engaging and playful, with potential for educational applications, but also revealed significant usability limitations, including low precision, environmental sensitivity, and higher cognitive load compared to a standard tablet interface.

Project Information:

  • Institution: Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e)
  • Course: Designing user interfaces with emerging technologies (DBM170)
  • Year: 2024
  • Team: Alexandra van Dijk, Guyon Crousen, Joey del Castilho, Yufei Song, Helena Mossel

What did I learn...

This DUIET group project focused on developing my ability to work more experimentally through the Paper Screen concept, driven by curiosity about how light interacts with paper. It showed me the value of curiosity-led exploration in generating unexpected ideas, while also teaching me the importance of balancing experimentation with clear direction to ensure meaningful outcomes.
